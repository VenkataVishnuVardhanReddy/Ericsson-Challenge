Ericsson Final Pitch Blueprint & Future-Proof Data Strategy

            
Structured directly around the 5 jury evaluation criteria in the Ericsson Hackathon brief.

          

        


        

          

            Bronze Tier · 30–90 Days
            
1. Raw Ingestion Layer

            

              SharePoint, OneDrive & Ericsson Play MP4s and PPTXs are watched via webhook. Before storage-quota deletion triggers, Distill automatically extracts canonical knowledge packets.
            

          

          

            Silver Tier · 20+ Year Archive
            
2. Open Knowledge Packets

            

              Vendor-neutral transcript.vtt, WebP keyframes, 16 kbps Opus speech, 8s speaker samples, and CAS slide pointers (SLD-*) — 98.5–99.9% smaller and playable/recreatable anytime.
            

          

          

            Gold Tier · AI & RAG Ready
            
3. Continuous AI Activation

            

              PII-redacted, deduplicated chunks.jsonl with full provenance (doc_number, revision, speaker, timestamp) feeding Ericsson internal LLMs & hybrid search.
            

          

        


        

          

            

              
✅ How We Addressed All 5 Ericsson Jury Criteria

            

            

              

                1. Use Cases Addressed: Plain + speaker-diarized transcription, webcam-masked slide keyframe capture, Ericsson doc-number (`6/221 09-FGB...`) & revision (`Rev A/B`) linking, text-to-slide prompts, on-demand MP4 recreation, 5-level deduplication, and safe leaver offboarding.
              

              

                2. Technical Architecture: 100% offline CPU pipeline combining faster-whisper (int8), sherpa-onnx speaker diarization, temporal variance webcam masking, RapidOCR, dual slide hashing (boilerplate-stripped content_id + pHash), and SQLite FTS5 + MiniLM vector RRF.
              

              

                3. Scenario-Based Volume Savings: Measured 98.3% reduction on Jury and prizes.mp4 with full playable Opus audio (88.65 MB → 1.51 MB), 98.89% for keyframes + transcript (0.99 MB), 99.9%+ for pure text + canonical slide links, and 42.6% delta savings across SD-WAN revisions.
              

              

                4. Future-Proof AI Data Strategy: Medallion Bronze/Silver/Gold lifecycle with Content-Addressable Slide Storage ("Git for Slides") and automated GDPR PII redaction before LLM training.
              

              

                5. Further Work & Open Challenges: Hybrid T1 video retention for non-slide live hardware demos/whiteboards, GPU cluster worker scaling (2-hr video in <90s), opt-in corporate voice-print directory, and Microsoft Graph ACL passthrough.
